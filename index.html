Upstream

Group Project

Roles: Programming, Design, Music, Writing

A physics-based platformer with a minimalist control scheme where the player controls a salmon who is trying to return to its place of birth in order to lay its eggs. Inspired by other games such as Getting Over it With Bennet Foddy, Celeste, Journey, and Never Alone, the game features challenging levels, a fun to use but hard to master control scheme, as well as an education and emotional narrative.

ConductVR

Group Project

Roles: Programming, Design, Sound, UI

The final culmination of my undergraduate degrees, and combination of my two education paths, this VR education tool will allow students to learn the art of conducting in a 3d space, with audio and visual aids to enhance the learning process. Conductor’s Podium: VR will also enable students of conducting to connect with their instructors for real-time, 3D, instruction from anywhere in the world. This project is currently in progress and scheduled for completion in Spring of 2024.

Dunk

Group Project

Roles: Gameplay, AI, Sound, Art

An atmospheric, first-person, horror game, with a 64×64 resolution, made for LowRezJam 2023. Players are trapped on the bottom of the ocean and must collect the broken parts of their submarine to escape. Multi-Dimensional Dic-Tac-Toe

Solo Project

A twist on the classic game of Tic-Tac-Toe that makes the game more engaging to play and less likely to result in a tie. I designed a 3D game mode which is just what it sounds like, Tic-Tac-Toe but with a 3×3×3 cube, as well as a “Multi-Dimensional” game mode. Multi-Dimensional mode starts as a 3D game, but every time a cube is selected, a flat or “normal” game is initiated, whoever wins the normal game, claims the cube. I also introduced “Override Moves” which allow players to play on top of their opponent in the flat game, and further shake up the pattern of traditional Tic-Tac-Toe.

I was born in Kotzebue, Alaska, but spent most of my life growing up in Juneau, Alaska. I played the viola and the violin throughout gradeschool and in my freshman year of high school, I was invited to join the Juneau Symphony Orchestra. Throughout my entire youth, I was also involved with the FIRST robotics program. I started in the FIRST Lego League (FLL) program in 1st grade, and then in high school, I joined the FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC). In FTC, I was the captain of my team for three years, and in my third year as captain, my team became the Alaska State Champions. We then went on to compete at the World Championships for that season, it was the first time anyone from Southeast Alaska had competed at that level.

From 2019-2024 I attended Pacific Lutheran University (PLU) in Tacoma, Washington. My two degrees are a Bachelor of Music in Music Education and Bachelor of Arts in Computer Science.

I am incredibly passionate about working in video game development after I graduate. I see video games as the perfect combination of all art forms, which include a level of audience interaction/agency that is not possible in any other media. I love storytelling, and no medium can tell stories or create memorable experiences quite like what is possible with video games.

Some of my biggest inspirations in terms of game design, narrative, and music include Journey (thatgamecompany), ABZU (Giant Squid), The Pathless (Giant Squid), The Beginner's Guide (Davey Wreden), Outer Wilds (Mobius Digital), Stardew Valley (ConcernedApe), Homeworld (Relic Entertainment), Subsurface Circular (Bithell Games), No Man's Sky (Hello Games), Portal (Valve), and The Elder Scrolls Series (Bethesda Game Studios).

In my free time I spend my time working on a number of different game-related projects, from participating in game jams, to now starting my own game studio.
